Root causes

1. The weaker models do not extract the stage's process from the PPAP

The golden covers all 15 control-plan operations (PP-010 to PP-150) in one WI. Only the Luna models reproduce them. The others each fail in a different way:

ModelOperations presentWhat it wrote insteadCompletion tokens
gpt-4o-miniIncoming, shearing, piercing, generic “forming”, all without valuesWelding-example fixture steps and rework rows700
mistral-smallRaw-material receipt onlyDocument, PDI and supplier-return steps729
Nano-5NoneGeneric assembly / spot welding, approval-box text903

None of these is a token-limit effect: gpt-4o-mini and mistral-small had 16,000 max_tokens and Nano-5 had 4,000. mistral-small also took a single operation on 289661208202 (PP-030 only).

2. The prompt's filled-in example still leaks into the output

The welding example in pcp_wi_generator/prompt_builder.py reappears here. gpt-4o-mini opens with “Ensure all loose parts are collected”, “Verify all assembly parts are loaded on the fixture” and “Clamp the fixture securely”, and adds spot / weld / part / nut-missing and part-shift rework rows. Nano-5 adds a fixture and spot welding. None of this is in the PPAP, which has no welding apart from the M6 nut projection.

“UNIT-3” appears in gpt-4o-mini, mistral-small and Nano-5 (as “UNIT III”), and also in the golden. The PPAP says UNIT-II in all 10 places it names the unit, so the golden's line area most likely came from the same example.

3. Header Stage No. and revision have no clear source

Stage No.: the golden says “Multiple Stages”, which is not in the PPAP. The five models gave five different answers: “1”, “1”, “RM RECEIPT”, “PP-010 to PP-150” and “1-15”. For both Luna models this is the only contradicted critical fact.

Revision: the golden uses the control plan's Rev 0 and date 09/5/18. The PPAP also carries the quality plan's Rev 01, the “/01” doc-number suffix and the part change level NR/14.03.2020. gpt-4o-mini and Nano-5 took 01. The three others took 0/00 and are correct.

4. The judge penalises detail that the PPAP supports

Critical additions found in the PPAP: Luna-5.6 20 of 20, Luna-6 12 of 12, mistral-small 7 of 8, Nano-5 4 of 10, gpt-4o-mini 1 of 8. The Luna models' additions are control-plan data the golden leaves out: min shut heights, tool and liner sizes, WP-PRD-17, FeZn8Cr III Y, DFT meter and per-hour sampling. Invented content is concentrated in gpt-4o-mini and Nano-5.

5. Golden rework rows cannot be derived from the PPAP

“Plastic mallet”, “rust remover”, “rust preventive” and “grinding or filing” appear nowhere in the PPAP. The PPAP reaction plans say “Sort / Reject”, “Sort / Reject & Sent back To Supplier” and “Resharpening / Replacement of stopper/Tool”. Models that follow the PPAP, like Luna-6 rejecting surface defects or Luna-5.6 returning rusted parts to the supplier, are marked CONTRADICTED. gpt-4o-mini gets the plastic-mallet row right only because it copies the example. Rework is the lowest-scoring section for both Luna models.

Recommendations

  1. Replace the filled-in welding example in prompt_builder.py with placeholders or a press-shop example, and tell the model never to copy example values. The same leakage showed up on 289661208202.
  2. Tell the generator to cover every control-plan operation that belongs to the stage, listing them before writing steps. mistral-small stopped at raw-material receipt here and at a single operation on 289661208202.
  3. Fix the header rules: agree with the golden author what Stage No. should hold when one WI covers all operations (“Multiple Stages”?), and say which revision to use (the control plan's, here Rev 0 and 09/5/18). Then put both rules in the prompt.
  4. Correct or confirm the golden: line area UNIT-3 vs the PPAP's UNIT-II, and the rework actions that are not in the PPAP. As it stands, the golden rewards copying the example.
  5. Add a source-supported class to the judge (e.g. SOURCE_SUPPORTED_ADDITION) for extra content that is traceable to the PPAP, so that it does not trip the hallucination gate. On this part, that alone would remove all 32 of the Luna models' critical additions.
  6. Keep testing Luna-5.6 and Luna-6 only. Luna-5.6 has led on 5 of the 6 stage-WIs scored so far (this part and the four stages of 290058600101), and Luna-6 on one (289661208202), at less than half the cost. If Luna-5.6 is kept, raise its max_tokens above 4,000. Decide after re-running with the fixed prompt; this report covers one WI per model.

Method

Judge. Each generated WI was compared with its golden WI (Hindi/WI_20260616__544283753302 PPAP 08.04.23_Stage_01.xlsx) using wi_judge_single_prompt.md. Claude applied the prompt; eval_tools/score.py computed every score from the verdicts, so the arithmetic is exact and auditable.

Golden facts. Each golden WI was split once into atomic facts, and the same list was used for every model. Each fact is MATCH (1.0), PARTIAL (0.5), MISSING (0) or CONTRADICTED (0), weighted 3 / 2 / 1 for critical / major / minor.

Pass rule. A model fails if any critical fact is contradicted, if there is any critical hallucination, or if critical recall is below 0.95.

Judgement calls applied to all models:

  • Stage No. “1-15” (Luna-5.6) and “PP-010 to PP-150” (Luna-6) are scored CONTRADICTED against “Multiple Stages”, under the runbook rule for operation codes. Accepting them would still leave both models failing on PPAP-backed critical additions.
  • Line area UNIT-II (Luna-5.6, Luna-6) is CONTRADICTED against the golden's UNIT-3, even though the PPAP supports UNIT-II, because the judge scores against the golden only. The fact is MINOR, so the effect is small.
  • “0” is accepted as revision “00”, and “09/5/18” as “09/05/18”.
  • The golden's “as per lab test report” check is PARTIAL wherever a model cites F-QAD/SI/RMQP or “approved criteria” instead. The RMQP reference itself is listed as a critical addition.
  • gpt-4o-mini's single generic “forming operations” block is credited once, to Forming I. Its Forming II and III settings and dimensions are MISSING.
  • Returning low-coating or defective plated parts to the plater is PARTIAL (Luna-6) unless the row also states re-coating and a thickness re-check (Luna-5.6, MATCH).

Limits. This covers one part, one stage and one generation per model. Treat the ranking as indicative until it is repeated on more WIs.
