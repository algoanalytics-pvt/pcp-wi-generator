Root causes

1. The weaker models do not extract the press process from the PPAP

The P-C-P lists 11 operations (PP-010 to PP-110), and the golden covers the incoming checks, shearing, the four press operations, inspection, storage and packing. Only the Luna models carry all of it. The other three fail in different ways:

ModelWhat it hasWhat it lacks or replacesCompletion tokens
mistral-smallAll press settings, shearing size, incoming checksEvery blanking, piercing and forming dimension1,725 of 16,000
gpt-4o-miniOutline of shearing, piercing, Forming I, without valuesBlanking, Forming II, all settings and dimensions608 of 4,000
Nano-5Header onlyThe whole process; writes a fixture assembly instead686 of 4,000

None of this is a token-limit effect: all three stopped far below their limits.

2. The prompt's filled-in example still leaks into the output

Nano-5 reproduces the welding example in pcp_wi_generator/prompt_builder.py almost verbatim: “Collect all loose parts and place them together”, “Clamp the fixture”, a fixture with a fixture number, a weld/attachment rework row, and the date 11/11/17. mistral-small writes “UNIT-3”, the example's line area; the PPAP says UNIT-II. (prompt_builder.py is not on this branch; the strings match the example documented in the runbook.)

11/11/17 is also the PFD form's revision date in this PPAP, so Nano-5's date could come from either source. The same leakage was reported on 289661208202 and 544283753302.

3. The golden leaves out control-plan rows, and the judge counts them as hallucinations

Critical additions found in the PPAP: Luna-5.6 19 of 20, Luna-6 14 of 14, mistral-small 4 of 8, gpt-4o-mini 2 of 2, Nano-5 1 of 6. The golden omits several P-C-P rows that the Luna models copy: the 28 mm piercing dimension, R3 on Forming I, M.Tape for shearing, min/max shut heights, threads, tool and liner sizes, F-PRD-17 and per-hour/per-lot sampling.

It also omits the change that created revision 02. The P-C-P change log for 06.03.2026 adds separate LH/RH bins with identification tags, and PP-070/PP-100 add RPO. The golden is at revision 02 but contains neither, so the models that include the rev-02 change are penalised for it.

4. Some golden values cannot be derived from the PPAP

The golden's ±0.05 mm sheet-thickness tolerance does not appear anywhere in the PPAP; the P-C-P gives only 1.5. Every model scores this critical fact PARTIAL at best. For Luna-5.6 it is one of only three non-MATCH critical facts: crediting it would raise its recall from 0.946 to 0.964, above the gate.

“Plastic mallet” and “rust remover” are also absent. The golden's “part missing → assemble all parts and inspect” row does not fit a single pressed part; only Nano-5 matches it, by copying the assembly example. The PPAP reaction plans say “Sort / Reject”, “Sort / Reject & Sent back To Supplier” and “Resharpening / Replacement of stopper/Tool”. Luna-6 follows them for rusty material and is marked CONTRADICTED. Rework is the lowest-scoring section for both Luna models.

5. Header and part-list rules are not explicit enough

Revision: the golden uses the P-C-P's (Rev.No.) 02 and 06.03.2026, not the form's own Rev 0 / 11/11/2017. Four models got this right; Nano-5 took 11/11/17.

Part No.: gpt-4o-mini and mistral-small appended the change level from “Part Number / Latest Change Level: 2654 7220 8237/8238N/H/06/01/12”.

Stage No.: Luna-6 wrote “PP-010 to PP-110”. It has written an operation-code range on every one of the 7 stage-WIs scored so far (289661208202, the four stages of 290058600101, 544283753302 and this part).

Part list: for this LH/RH pair the golden has one row per part number with quantity 1. Luna-6 and Nano-5 merged them into one row, and mistral-small dropped the N from 8238N.

Recommendations

  1. Replace the filled-in welding example in prompt_builder.py with placeholders or a press-shop example, and tell the model never to copy example values. It leaks here (Nano-5 verbatim; mistral-small's UNIT-3), as it did on 289661208202 and 544283753302.
  2. Add explicit header rules to the prompt: Stage No. is the stage number, never an operation-code range (Luna-6 has got this wrong on all 7 stage-WIs). Part No. is written without the change level. Revision and date come from the control plan's (Rev.No.) / Date (Rev.) fields, not the form's own revision. An LH/RH pair gets one part-list row per part number, each with its quantity.
  3. Tell the generator to give every dimension listed for each operation, not “as per control plan”. mistral-small has every setting but no dimensions.
  4. Review the golden with its author: the source of the ±0.05 mm tolerance, the plastic-mallet, rust-remover and part-missing rework rows, and whether the rev-02 LH/RH bin segregation, RPO, the 28 mm piercing dimension and R3 on Forming I should be in the WI. As it stands, the golden rewards copying the example and penalises reading the PPAP.
  5. Add a source-supported class to the judge (e.g. SOURCE_SUPPORTED_ADDITION) for extra content traceable to the PPAP, so that it does not trip the hallucination gate. On this part, that would remove 19 of Luna-5.6's 20 critical additions and all 14 of Luna-6's.
  6. Keep testing Luna-5.6 and Luna-6 only. Luna-5.6 has now led on 6 of the 7 stage-WIs scored (this part, 544283753302 and the four stages of 290058600101), and Luna-6 on one (289661208202), at half the cost. Raise max_tokens above 4,000 for both: Luna-6 used 3,813 here. Decide after re-running with the fixed prompt; this report covers one WI per model.

Method

Judge. Each generated WI was compared with its golden WI (Hindi/WI_20260616_265472208237 NEW PPAP_Stage_01.xlsx) using wi_judge_single_prompt.md. Claude applied the prompt; eval_tools/score.py computed every score from the verdicts, so the arithmetic is exact and auditable.

Golden facts. Each golden WI was split once into atomic facts, and the same list was used for every model. Each fact is MATCH (1.0), PARTIAL (0.5), MISSING (0) or CONTRADICTED (0), weighted 3 / 2 / 1 for critical / major / minor.

Pass rule. A model fails if any critical fact is contradicted, if there is any critical hallucination, or if critical recall is below 0.95.

Judgement calls applied to all models:

  • Forming I lists 26 mm twice and Forming II lists 25 mm twice. These are scored as separate facts because the P-C-P has them as separate characteristics. Luna-6 lists 26 mm once, so one of them is MISSING.
  • A merged LH/RH part-list row (Luna-6, Nano-5) is PARTIAL for every part-list fact. Luna-6's quantity “As applicable (LH/RH)” is PARTIAL (field present without a number) rather than CONTRADICTED.
  • mistral-small lists its machines only in the tooling list, and its header machine field says “Manual”. The header machine-list facts are PARTIAL; the operation→machine bindings are MATCH via the tooling list.
  • The UNIT-II → UNIT-III transport (Luna-5.6, Luna-6) is a new process step and listed as a critical addition, not as the golden's dispatch. Luna-6 packs but never dispatches, so its pack-and-dispatch fact is PARTIAL.
  • The golden's “check the lab report” step is PARTIAL when the lab report is not named (both Luna models), matching the rule used on 544283753302.
  • Rejecting or replacing a dented part is PARTIAL, not CONTRADICTED, because the golden's action is “fix with plastic mallet or replace the part”.
  • mistral-small's “apply chamfer 5×45° and R3 after cutting” is CONTRADICTED: the values are bound to shearing instead of to the blanking check.

Limits. This covers one part, one stage and one generation per model. Treat the ranking as indicative until it is repeated on more WIs.
