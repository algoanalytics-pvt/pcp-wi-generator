WI Generator: Model Comparison

Part 290058600101 · BKT ASSY JACK MTG · Stages 01, 02, 03, 04 · Source: PPAP 290058600101 .xls · Evaluated 10 November 2026
Summary
  • No model passes the judge on any stage (0 of 20 WIs). Every WI fails at least one gate: a contradicted critical fact, a critical hallucination, or critical recall below 0.95.
  • Luna-5.6 leads (overall 0.930, critical recall 0.948), with Luna-6 second (0.883, 0.943). Both reproduce every golden operation and process value in all four stages: the welding parameters, press bindings, dimensions, angles, radii, coating and tapping values. Every critical fact they contradict is a header field (stage number, dates, revision). mistral-small is a distant third (0.665), and gpt-4o-mini (0.442) and Nano-5 (0.322) are not usable.
  • Luna-6 costs about the same as gpt-4o-mini ($0.0038 vs $0.0036 per WI). Luna-5.6 costs twice as much ($0.0077) for +0.047 overall. Nano-5 is the cheapest ($0.0016), but its output can't be used.
  • The weak models copy the generator prompt's spot-welding example or dump raw PCP cells instead of writing a WI. Two examples: gpt-4o-mini's “Clamp the fixture” steps and Nano-5's “Gun No. 90”. For the Luna models the opposite happens: 84 of their 85 critical hallucinations are real PPAP data that the golden leaves out.
  • The golden has problems of its own that every model is penalised for. “UNIT-3” is not in the PPAP; it comes from the prompt example. The golden Stage No. values “Multiple” (Stage 02) and “1-5” (Stage 04) follow no rule, so all five models contradict them. Stages 03 and 04 also take their header dates from different PPAP fields. Read the pass/fail gates with this in mind: if PPAP-backed additions were exempt, Luna-5.6's Stage 01 would pass.

Root causes

1. The prompt's filled-in welding example leaks into the output

The _SCHEMA_EXAMPLE in pcp_wi_generator/prompt_builder.py (from line 48) is a complete spot-welding WI for COVER ASSY FUEL FILTER. Weaker models copy it instead of extracting from the PPAP. This happened on 289661208202 too.

The genuinely invented additions are few and come from the weaker models: welding content in press stages, the spiral clamping fixture, drilling rework, and a ±0.001 mm tolerance.

4. Golden rework rows cannot be derived from the PPAP

“Plastic mallet” and “rust remover” appear nowhere in the PPAP, yet the golden uses them in every stage. Rework (weight 0.15) is the weakest section for every model: Luna-5.6 averages 0.70, mistral-small 0.46, Luna-6 0.38, gpt-4o-mini 0.22 and Nano-5 0.21. The score mostly measures whether a model reproduces house wording, not whether it read the source.

5. Token limits do not explain the weak outputs

Luna-5.6 and Nano-5 ran with max_tokens 4,000, the others with 16,000. The average completion per WI was 586 tokens for gpt-4o-mini, 1,293 for Nano-5, 1,331 for mistral-small, 2,810 for Luna-5.6 and 3,352 for Luna-6. Nano-5 is far below its limit, and Luna-5.6, the model closest to its limit, produced the most complete WIs. The weak results come from the prompt and the model, not from truncation.

Recommendations

  1. Replace the filled-in example in prompt_builder.py with placeholders, or with a neutral press-shop example, and tell the model never to copy example values. This is the top action across both evaluated parts.
  2. Add explicit header rules to the prompt: take the revision and date from the part's own PCP block (Rev.No. and Date(Rev), falling back to Date(Orig)), never from the form's “Rev. Date”. Set Stage No. to the WI stage number, not PCP operation codes, and spell out the factory/line area format.
  3. Review the golden for 290058600101 before relying on these scores: “UNIT-3” (not in the PPAP), Stage No. “Multiple” and “1-5”, the Stage 04 date 11/11/17 against the PCP's 10.12.2022, and the plastic-mallet and rust-remover rework rows. After any fix, update golden_facts_Stage_NN.json and the affected verdicts, then rerun score.py.
  4. Add a source-supported class to the judge (e.g. SOURCE_SUPPORTED_ADDITION) for extras traced to the PPAP, and keep the hallucination gate only for content found in neither the golden nor the PPAP. With that change, Luna-5.6's Stage 01 would pass.
  5. Keep testing Luna-6 and Luna-5.6 only. Their order has flipped between parts: on 289661208202 Luna-6 led with 0.873 against 0.853, and here Luna-5.6 leads with 0.930 against 0.883. The gap between them is within the variation seen so far. Luna-6 costs half as much. Drop gpt-4o-mini and Nano-5. These results come from two parts and five WIs per model, one generation each.

Method

Judge. Each generated WI was compared with its golden WI (Hindi/WI_20260616_PPAP 290058600101 _Stage_01.xlsx, Hindi/WI_20260616_PPAP 290058600101 _Stage_02.xlsx, Hindi/WI_20260616_PPAP 290058600101 _Stage_03.xlsx, Hindi/WI_20260616_PPAP 290058600101 _Stage_04.xlsx) using wi_judge_single_prompt.md. Claude applied the prompt; eval_tools/score.py computed every score from the verdicts, so the arithmetic is exact and auditable.

Golden facts. Each golden WI was split once into atomic facts, and the same list was used for every model. Each fact is MATCH (1.0), PARTIAL (0.5), MISSING (0) or CONTRADICTED (0), weighted 3 / 2 / 1 for critical / major / minor.

Pass rule. A model fails if any critical fact is contradicted, if there is any critical hallucination, or if critical recall is below 0.95.

Judgement calls applied to all models:

  • Golden facts were taken as written, even where they look wrong (UNIT-3, Stage No. “Multiple” or “1-5”, the Stage 04 date 11/11/17), because the judge prompt forbids correcting the golden.
  • A correct stage name with extra text appended is PARTIAL. Operations given in place of the stage name are CONTRADICTED. Revision “0” against “00” is a MATCH.
  • A rework row that rejects a part where the golden repairs it is CONTRADICTED. A row that keeps the repair but drops the golden's “or reject” option, or gives a vague action, is PARTIAL.
  • In Stage 01, “3 tacks per nut” against the golden's “6 tacks on M8 nut” is PARTIAL. A machine named only in the steps or tooling list, but not in the header machine list, is PARTIAL for the header fact.
  • Content not in the golden counts as a hallucination even when the PPAP supports it, as the judge prompt requires (see the root cause on PPAP-supported detail).

Limits. Four stages of one part, one generation per model. Together with 289661208202 this makes two parts, so treat the ranking as indicative.
