Root causes

1. The golden leaves out control-plan rows, and the judge counts them as hallucinations

The golden follows the PCP's operations PP-010 to PP-130 but keeps only part of each row. It omits the max/min shut heights, strokes and pressures for every press, the piercing CD dimensions (60, 84, 26, 43, 31, 49), 15 / 12.4 and the 9×15 and 20×12 slots, the Forming-I angle 120.75°, Forming-II's 8°, 21.5 and 69, the tool sizes, and F-PRD-17 and TS10812. Its piercing step even says “Ø8 × 3, Ø7 × 1 and other dimensions”.

It also omits the PCP's own revision-02 change: green-dot marking at the PDI gate to confirm the M6 nut after coating. Luna-6 includes this step and is penalised for it.

The better a model reads the PPAP, the more critical additions it gets.

2. Some golden values cannot be derived from the PPAP

Thickness tolerance: the golden QI says “3 mm ±0.001 mm”. The PPAP says “3 MM” and “Micrometer [LC=0.001mm]”, so the golden appears to have turned the least count into a tolerance. Every model loses this fact. Without it, both Luna models' critical recall would be 0.984.

Rework: “plastic mallet” does not appear anywhere in the PPAP. “Rust removal” appears only as an FMEA detection control. Only Luna-5.6 names the mallet; the others reject cracks or give vague actions.

3. The prompt's filled-in example still leaks into the weaker models

gpt-4o-mini opens with “Collect all loose parts and place them together”, “Verify all assembly parts are loaded on fixture” and “Clamp the fixture”. Nano-5 adds “Spot Welding Gun - 1 no. (Gun No. 90)” and a welding fixture. None of these is in this part's process: the PPAP has projection welding and CO2 tacking, but no spot welding and no fixture loading.

“UNIT-3” in mistral-small, gpt-4o-mini and Nano-5 matches the golden here, and the PPAP supports it (“UNIT-III”). It is not evidence of leakage on this part.

Recommendations

  1. Add a source-supported class to the judge (e.g. SOURCE_SUPPORTED_ADDITION) for extra content that is traceable to the PPAP, so that it does not trip the hallucination gate. On this part, that alone would leave Luna-5.6 with no failed gate.
  2. Review the golden with its author: the ±0.001 mm tolerance (likely the micrometer least count), the plastic-mallet action, the missing piercing CD dimensions and slots, and the rev-02 green-dot PDI step. As it stands, the golden penalises reading the PPAP.
  3. Add explicit header rules to the prompt: Stage No. is the stage number, never an operation-code range (Luna-6 has now done this on all 8 stage-WIs scored). Line area is the unit, not the company name. Revision and date come from the control plan's (Rev.No.) / Date (Rev.) fields, not the form's “Rev. Date”.
  4. Replace the filled-in welding example in prompt_builder.py with placeholders or a neutral example, and tell the model never to copy example values. It leaks into gpt-4o-mini and Nano-5 again here, as on 289661208202, 544283753302 and 265472208237.
  5. Keep testing Luna-5.6 and Luna-6 only. Luna-5.6 has now led on 7 of the 8 stage-WIs scored, and Luna-6 on one (289661208202), at half the cost. On this part the gap is only 0.023. If Luna-5.6 is kept, raise its max_tokens above 4,000. This report covers one generation per model, so decide after re-running with the fixed prompt and judge.

Method

Judge. Each generated WI was compared with its golden WI (Hindi/WI_20260616_511830100101_PPAP 21.01.26_Stage_01.xlsx) using wi_judge_single_prompt.md. Claude applied the prompt; eval_tools/score.py computed every score from the verdicts, so the arithmetic is exact and auditable.

Golden facts. Each golden WI was split once into atomic facts, and the same list was used for every model. Each fact is MATCH (1.0), PARTIAL (0.5), MISSING (0) or CONTRADICTED (0), weighted 3 / 2 / 1 for critical / major / minor.

Pass rule. A model fails if any critical fact is contradicted, if there is any critical hallucination, or if critical recall is below 0.95.

Judgement calls applied to all models:

  • Header machines and tooling items are credited wherever they appear in the generated WI. For Nano-5, this includes the raw PCP cells in its step table.
  • Shut heights given without a unit (mistral-small “shut height (630)”, Nano-5 “SHUT HEIGHT | 630”) are PARTIAL, under the runbook's unit-dropped rule.
  • mistral-small's 1270 ±3 × 304 ±3 appears only as a raw-material receipt check, and its shearing step says “to achieve required dimensions”. Both shear facts are PARTIAL, not MATCH or CONTRADICTED.
  • Dimensions that appear only in a model's finished-QC list (mistral-small) are credited to the matching step facts. Generic “dimensions as per specification / PCP” (gpt-4o-mini, Nano-5) is PARTIAL for the QF dimension facts only.
  • “Check lab test report” is PARTIAL wherever a model cites F-QAD/SI/RMQP instead, as on 544283753302. Luna-6's step 2 sends samples to the lab, so its step fact is a MATCH and its QI fact is PARTIAL.
  • Rework rows that reject a defect the golden repairs are CONTRADICTED only when no repair row exists for that defect (mistral-small). When a model has both a reject row and a vague repair row (Luna-6), they are PARTIAL.
  • The Doc No. keeps the PPAP's stray “`'” prefix in the golden and in all five outputs, and is a MATCH for all. “13/11/2025” is accepted as “13.11.2025”.
  • RPO application, the powder-coating set-up and the “M6 Nut STD” reference are counted as critical additions (a new step, a new machine and a new document reference). They are counted the same way for every model.

Limits. This covers one part, one stage and one generation per model. Treat the ranking, especially the 0.023 gap between the two Luna models, as indicative until it is repeated on more WIs.
