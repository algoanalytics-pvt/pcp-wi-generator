Summary
  • 40 WIs were judged: 5 models × 8 stage-WIs from 5 parts (one part with 4 stages, four with 1). None passes the judge. Every WI fails at least one gate: a contradicted critical fact, a critical hallucination, or critical recall below 0.95.
  • Luna-5.6 is the strongest model. It has a mean overall score of 0.900, is best on 7 of 8 WIs and 4 of 5 parts, and is the only model whose pooled critical recall clears the 0.95 gate (0.952). Luna-6 is a close second (0.867). It wins on 289661208202 and costs less than half as much ($0.0041 vs $0.0085 per WI).
  • The other three models are far behind. mistral-small averages 0.566, but its scores range from 0.27 to 0.93 because it often stops after one operation. gpt-4o-mini (0.401) and Nano-5 (0.256) write English on every part and copy the generator prompt's welding example instead of reading the PPAP.
  • Almost none of the Luna models' failures are in the process content. Every critical fact they contradict is a header field (Stage No., revision or date). Of the critical additions that were checked against the PPAP, 193 of 195 are real PPAP data that the golden leaves out. If PPAP-backed additions were exempt, Luna-5.6 would pass 2 of its 7 tagged WIs, and clear header rules would remove most of the remaining failures.
  • The prompt, the judge and the golden WIs need fixing more than the model choice does. Remove the filled-in example from prompt_builder.py, write explicit header rules, let the judge accept additions that come from the PPAP, and review the golden WIs with their author. Then re-run all five parts.
Recommendation

Use Luna-5.6 as the lead model. It is ahead on 7 of 8 WIs and is the only model with pooled critical recall above the gate. At $0.0085 per WI, it costs about $0.44 more per 100 WIs than Luna-6, which is small next to the cost of an operator following a wrong WI. Keep Luna-6 as the cost alternative: its process content is as good, and most of its gap comes from the Stage No. header error, which a prompt rule should fix. Drop gpt-4o-mini, Nano-5 and mistral-small. Make the final decision only after re-running both Luna models with the fixed prompt and judge.

Recurring root causes

1. The prompt's filled-in welding example leaks into the output (all 5 parts)

The JSON example in prompt_builder.py is a complete spot-welding WI for a different part. gpt-4o-mini and Nano-5 copy it on every part. mistral-small copies its “UNIT-3” line area, and Luna-5.6 took its 11/11/17 date on 289661208202. Some golden WIs contain the same example values (“UNIT-3”, plastic mallet, rust remover), so copying the example is rewarded.

2. Header fields have no explicit rules

The PPAP has up to three candidate revisions and dates: the part's, the control plan's and the PCP form's. The prompt does not say which one to use, or what Stage No. should hold when one WI covers several operations. As a result, all 21 critical contradictions from the Luna models are header fields, and the golden WIs are inconsistent here too.

3. The judge counts PPAP-supported detail as hallucination

The judge compares only against the golden WI. Control-plan rows that the golden leaves out are counted as critical hallucinations even when they are in the PPAP, and this is 193 of the 195 tagged additions from the Luna models. Because of this, the hallucination gate cannot tell the best models from the worst: every model fails it on every WI.

4. Some golden values cannot be derived from the PPAP

Some golden values do not appear in the source PPAP: line area “UNIT-3” (the PPAP says UNIT-II), the Stage No. values “Multiple”, “1-5” and “Multiple Stages”, the ±0.05 mm and ±0.001 mm tolerances, and the plastic-mallet, rust-remover and rust-preventive rework rows. Rework is the weakest section for every model; the best score is 0.645.

5. The weaker models do not extract the process

gpt-4o-mini writes about 600 output tokens per WI and Nano-5 about 1,100, against 3,100 to 3,500 for the Luna models. The token limit does not explain this, because they stop well before it. mistral-small often covers only the first operation of the stage. These models summarise or copy the prompt instead of turning the control plan into steps.

Recommendations

  1. Fix the prompt example. Replace the filled-in welding WI in prompt_builder.py with placeholders or a neutral press-shop example, and tell the model never to copy example values.
  2. Add header rules to the prompt. Stage No. is the WI's stage number, never an operation-code range. Revision and date come from the control plan's Rev.No. / Date (Rev.), not from the form. Line area is the unit given in the PPAP. An LH/RH pair gets one part-list row per part number.
  3. Fix the judge. Add a source-supported class (e.g. SOURCE_SUPPORTED_ADDITION) for additions found in the PPAP, and keep the hallucination gate for content that is in neither the golden nor the PPAP. Re-tag 289661208202, which was judged before PPAP tagging was added.
  4. Review the golden WIs with their author: UNIT-3, the Stage No. values, the tolerances, the rework rows, and the control-plan rows that the golden leaves out. Then update golden_facts_Stage_NN.json and re-score.
  5. Model choice: make Luna-5.6 the lead model and keep Luna-6 as the lower-cost alternative. Drop gpt-4o-mini, Nano-5 and mistral-small. Set max_tokens to 16,000 for both Luna models.
  6. Re-run all five parts with the fixed prompt and judge, using at least 3 generations per model, before making the final choice. The current gap between the two Luna models (0.033) is within the variation between parts.

Method and sources

Each part was evaluated separately with the runbook in WI_EVAL_RUNBOOK.md: every golden WI was split into atomic facts, Claude applied wi_judge_single_prompt.md to each generated WI, and eval_tools/score.py computed the scores from the verdicts. This summary re-reads the same judge_result_*.json files with eval_tools/build_summary.py; no verdict was changed. Facts are weighted 3 / 2 / 1 (critical / major / minor) and sections Steps 0.35, Finished QC 0.15, Rework 0.15, Incoming QC 0.10, Header 0.10, Tooling 0.10, Legend 0.05. A WI passes only with 0 contradicted critical facts, 0 critical hallucinations and critical recall of at least 0.95.

Limits. Five parts, 8 stage-WIs and one generation per model, scored by one judge pass. Generation settings were not uniform: max_tokens was 4,000 on some runs and 16,000 on others. The golden WIs have known problems (see root cause 4) that lower every model's score. Treat the gap between the two Luna models as indicative. The gap between the Luna models and the other three is large and consistent on every part. An earlier embedding-based comparison (MODEL_SUMMARY_ALL_PCPs.docx, 30 Oct) gave the same top three in the same order: Luna-5.6, Luna-6, mistral-small. Full findings, evidence and judgement calls are in each part's own report (listed under Results by part).
